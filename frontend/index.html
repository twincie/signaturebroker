Questions, answered

Insurance, without
the fine-print feeling.

How are the quotes calculated? +

Each quote uses the pricing model configured for that insurer and product. This may be a fixed premium, a percentage of the insured value, or an age-based rate.

How quickly can I get covered? +

Timing depends on the product and insurer. A displayed quote is an estimate until the selected insurer confirms the details, accepts payment and issues the policy.

Which insurance companies are available? +

The quote form uses active insurers and rates configured by the administrator. Available insurers may differ between products.

Can I submit a claim here? +

Not yet. Use the claims guidance above and contact the insurer shown on your policy. Online claims submission will be added later.
